The page you keep hunting for in search is now one click away. Hit the **star** on any page — from a row on your dashboard or from the top bar while you're reading it — and it pins to a new **Starred** tab.

- **Star from wherever you are** — the row on the dashboard, or the top bar of the page you're already reading
- **Starred is always there**, second tab along, so you can find it before you've starred anything
- **Newest star first** — the tab is ordered by when *you* pinned each page, not when it was created
- **Your stars are yours.** Nobody can see what you've starred, and starring never changes who can open a page
- **If you can open it, you can star it** — including your own private pages and private pages someone shared with you

Star a page you later lose access to and it quietly drops out of the tab — if you get access again, it comes right back.

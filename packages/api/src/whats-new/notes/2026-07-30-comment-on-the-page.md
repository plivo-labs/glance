Saying something about a page as a whole — *"this chart is wrong"*, *"ship it"*, *"who owns this?"* — used to mean picking some arbitrary sentence to hang the comment off. Now the page itself takes comments.

- **Add a comment** from the comments panel without selecting anything first
- **A button, not a box that's always open** — it's there when you want it and out of the way when you don't
- **Selecting text still works exactly as before.** This is another way in, not a replacement

Audio pages have had this button all along, because a waveform has no text to select. Now every page does.
